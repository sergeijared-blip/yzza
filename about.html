For You, Gorgeous ♡

A letter explaining how much I adore you

Oh hey there, gorgeous. It’s October 1, the day where guys are flexing their girls, and here I am, instead of just posting you, writing you a whole letter. I could literally post your pictures and say a thousand things about you, but honestly, I feel like words hit different when they come straight from the heart. Thank you for staying with me, especially during those moments na ang hirap kong intindihin and even I don’t know how to explain myself properly. Thank you for being patient with me, for understanding my moods, my silence, and even the parts of me na medyo complicated. Another month with you again, and honestly, I still feel so lucky na I get to have someone like you in my life.

I seriously cannot explain how much I adore you. Like, it’s not just because you’re pretty or because you have the kind of smile that literally makes everything feel lighter. It’s the way you laugh, the way your eyes look, your hair, the way you talk, the little things you do without even realizing them—everything about you just makes me soft. Napaka-ganda mo, sobra. If you could only see yourself through my eyes, grabe, you would understand why I sometimes just stare at you and think, “How did I get this lucky?” You’re beautiful inside and out, and what makes you even more beautiful is the heart behind that face. You have this kind of beauty na hindi lang nakikita, but something that you genuinely feel.

Thank you for trusting me with the things you don't usually tell everyone. I know trust isn't something na basta-basta binibigay, kaya I genuinely appreciate every little thing you choose to share with me. Your stories, your worries, your random thoughts, your fears, even the things you think are too small to matter—I care about all of them because they’re a part of you. And I promise, I’ll treasure that trust instead of taking it for granted. I might not always know exactly what to say, and sometimes baka sablay pa yung advice ko, but I’ll always try to listen first, understand you properly, and be there without making you feel like you have to explain yourself a hundred times.

I’m genuinely so proud of you, and I hope you know that. I know there are days na you feel tired, lost, or parang you're not doing enough, but please don't be too hard on yourself. You’re doing better than you think. I’m proud of you not because you have everything figured out, but because kahit ang daming things na dumadaan sa buhay mo, you still keep going. You keep trying, you keep learning, and you keep becoming someone stronger. And honestly, everything in this world may be high-priced, but my love for you is definitely higher. No amount of money, gifts, or expensive things could ever compare to how much I value you and how grateful I am that you’re part of my life.

And you know what? I don't expect our relationship to be perfect all the time. We’re both human, and obviously, we’ll have our moments. May times na magkaka-misunderstanding tayo, may times na magiging emotional tayo, and may moments na we won't immediately understand each other. But I hope we never let one bad moment make us forget all the good ones. I want us to learn how to talk things out instead of giving up, to understand instead of assuming, and to choose patience kahit minsan mahirap. I don't want something that only feels good when everything is perfect. I want something real enough to stay meaningful even during the messy parts. Because for me, love isn't just about butterflies and sweet words; it's also about choosing to understand each other when things aren't as easy as they used to be.

Amore mio, voglio che tu sappia quanto sei importante per me. Non sei semplicemente una persona che amo; sei qualcuno che ha lasciato una parte di sé nel mio cuore. Ti ringrazio per la pazienza, per la fiducia e per tutti quei piccoli momenti che forse per te sembrano semplici, ma che per me significano tantissimo. Non prometto che sarò sempre perfetto, perché so di avere ancora molte cose da imparare, ma prometto di continuare a scegliere te con sincerità, rispetto e amore. Se un giorno ti sentirai insicura del tuo valore, spero che tu possa ricordare che, ai miei occhi, sei molto più preziosa di quanto le parole possano descrivere. Ti amo non solo per ciò che sei quando tutto va bene, ma anche per la persona che sei nei giorni difficili. E finché la vita ci permetterà di condividere questi momenti, voglio continuare a creare ricordi con te, un giorno alla volta.

And honestly, I don't just love the big moments with you. I love the random little things too. Yung random conversations natin na wala namang point pero somehow, ang saya pa rin. Yung mga jokes na tayo lang nakakagets, yung random updates mo, yung simpleng “tumatae ako” or “kumakain ako,” hahahaha and even those moments na wala tayong masyadong sinasabi but somehow, comfortable pa rin. Those little things mean so much to me because they make me realize na love doesn't always have to be grand. Sometimes, love is just being there, checking on each other, remembering the small details, and making each other feel appreciated. I want to keep knowing you deeper—not just the version of you that everyone sees, but also the quiet version of you that only comes out when you feel safe.

And lastly, I want you to know na I’m not here just for the good days. I’m here for the ordinary days, the confusing days, the stressful days, and even those days na you don't feel like yourself. I don't want to be someone who only loves you when you're happy and easy to understand. I want to appreciate you as a whole person, with all your moods, dreams, flaws, fears, and little imperfections. I want to see you grow, chase your dreams, become the person you want to be, and be genuinely happy with the life you're building. And if I can be one of the people who makes that journey a little lighter for you, then honestly, that already means a lot to me. You don't have to be perfect for me to appreciate you. You just have to be you.

So, happy October 1, gorgeous. Another month, another chapter, and another reason for me to be thankful that you exist in my life. I know I may not always have the perfect words, the biggest surprises, or the most expensive gifts, but I hope you can always feel the sincerity behind everything I do. If I could give you anything, I’d honestly give you the ability to see yourself the way I see you, because maybe then you’d finally understand why I think you’re so damn beautiful. You’re precious to me in ways I probably can’t fully explain, and I hope I never make you question that. Thank you for staying, for understanding me, for trusting me, and for letting me be a part of your life. I don't know exactly what the future holds for us, but for now, I’m just genuinely grateful that I get to experience another month with you. And since it’s October 1, let me proudly say it: out of everyone I could write a letter for today, I’m choosing you. Always you.

Always you. ♡

Written with love, sincerity, and a little bit of cheesiness.
